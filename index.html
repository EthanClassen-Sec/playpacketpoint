Arcade FPS · Android

PACKETPOINT

Colorful arcade FPS where gunfights and cyberwarfare happen at the same time. Take a lane, then turn the map’s turrets, cameras, and doors against the other side.

  • Offline matches
  • Against bots
  • Guest or account
  • No ads
  • Touch or controller

Operations

Six ways to drop in. Score, tickets, or waves. You are back in the fight a few seconds after you drop.

Data Heist

Crack the vault. Carry the data. Upload it.

A sealed Data Vault comes online. Crack its ICE, carry the Data Core to an uplink, and hold the upload. First to 3 exfiltrations.

Team Deathmatch

Pure firefight. Hack the turrets, own the lanes.

First team to 40 eliminations. Turrets, cameras, and doors are live. Hijack them to swing a fight.

Breach

Attack and defend the server stacks.

Axiom attacks, Vandal defends two server clusters behind heavy ICE. Attackers spend respawn tickets. EMP and Breach Protocol crack servers faster.

Network Control

Three nodes. Hack them. Hold them.

Capture three relay nodes. Held nodes stream points and speed up bandwidth. First to 150.

Zombie Survival

Endless waves of infected. Hold out together.

A squad holds against nanite-infected waves. Match points buy weapons, perks, ammo, and a mystery cache. Hack the turrets so they fight for you.

Training Range

Test weapons, gadgets, and every hackable device.

No score and no clock. Infinite ammo, targets, hackable devices, and optional sparring bots.

Wardrobe

Dress the operator. Matches pay BITS, a soft currency with no cash value, for cosmetics. Two optional packs on Google Play unlock specific pieces. The whole game is playable without buying either.
